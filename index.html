    Audio Training

    Tutorial playlists for the Audio Engineer role.

      Why the Power Order Matters (X32 + Rack + Speakers)

      Powering gear on/off in the wrong order sends a pop or thump through the signal chain that gets amplified out to the speakers — repeated enough times, it degrades or damages drivers. The rule: the mixer goes on first and off last; amplifiers and powered speakers go on last and off first.

      Power ON: Board (X32) → Rack (processor) → Speakers & Subs
      Power OFF: Speakers & Subs → Rack (processor) → Board (X32)

      Also mute channels/bring the master down before powering off, and give the amps a couple seconds after power-down before cutting the rack — they store energy and need to discharge. On the X32, use Safe Shutdown when prompted so settings save cleanly.
